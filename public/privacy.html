Islamabad Runner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Islamabad Runner ("the game") is a free, offline endless-runner game. We take your privacy seriously, and the simplest way to protect it is to not collect anything.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, high scores, unlocked characters, settings and mission progress) is saved locally on your device using the app's private storage. It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game requests VIBRATE (for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings) and INTERNET (required by the WebView runtime; the game itself makes no network requests).

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and contains no chat, purchases, ads or external links other than this policy.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/oyekamal/islamabad-runner.
