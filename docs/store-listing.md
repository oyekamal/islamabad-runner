**Full description (4000):**
Run, jump and roll through the streets of Islamabad in a fast, colourful 3D endless runner!

The traffic warden and his dog are on your tail. Swipe to switch lanes, leap over barriers, roll under sign gantries and sprint across the roofs of Pakistan Railways coaches and bright red Metro buses. Grab coins, power-ups and mystery boxes as the city rushes past – from the graffiti walls of Blue Area to the Margalla foothills and the Metro corridor.

FEATURES
• Fast one-thumb controls: swipe left/right, up to jump, down to roll, double-tap for your hoverboard
• Endless, procedurally generated track that gets faster and trickier the further you run
• Power-ups: Jetpack, Super Sneakers, Coin Magnet and 2X Multiplier – all upgradable
• Hoverboards that save you from one crash, plus Headstarts and Score Boosters
• 7 characters to unlock, each with their own look
• 6 hoverboards with special abilities (Lowrider slides under barriers, Bouncer jumps higher)
• 50 mission sets that raise your score multiplier up to 30x
• Daily Word Hunt with streak rewards
• Keys to revive when you get caught
• Local leaderboard and stats
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, works fully offline

Landmarks you'll run past: Faisal Mosque, Centaurus, Pakistan Monument, the Metro Bus, chai dhabas, rickshaws and the container stacks of D-Chowk.

Made with love in Islamabad.
