**Full description (4000):**
Ride, jump and duck through the streets of Islamabad in a fast, colourful 3D endless runner!

It's lockdown day in the capital. The roads are blocked with shipping containers, the rangers are out with their batons, army jeeps are coming the other way and the mobile internet is down. You're a courier on a Honda 70 – and you're going to D-Chowk anyway.

Swipe to switch lanes, jump police barricades, duck under ROAD CLOSED signs and teargas, ride up dirt ramps onto container roofs and hop from truck to bus as the city rushes past – from the greenbelts of Faisal Avenue through the Srinagar Chokepoint and the Red Zone to the final sprint into D-Chowk.

FEATURES
• Fast one-thumb controls: swipe left/right, up to jump, down to duck, double-tap for Turbo
• Endless procedurally generated road that gets faster and trickier the further you ride
• Ride on top of containers, container trucks and Metro buses; dodge oncoming army jeeps
• Power-ups: Jetpack, Nitro Springs, Coin Magnet and 2X Multiplier – all upgradable
• Signal bubbles charge your Turbo, which saves you from one crash
• Biryani Boxes full of coins, keys, Turbos and character tokens
• 7 riders and 6 bikes to unlock, each with their own look and perks
• 50 mission sets that raise your score multiplier up to 30x
• Daily Word Hunt with streak rewards, keys to revive, local leaderboard
• Islamabad landmarks: Faisal Mosque, Centaurus, Pakistan Monument, the Metro Bus, chai dhabas, rickshaws and the container stacks of D-Chowk
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, works fully offline
