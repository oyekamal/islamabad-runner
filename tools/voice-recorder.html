🎙️ Islamabad Runner — record your voice

Say each line in your own voice, with energy. Keep each one short, roughly half a second to two seconds. Record in a quiet room, phone or laptop mic is fine. Hit record, say it, hit stop, listen back, redo if you want.

When you are happy with them all, press Download all. Put the files in public/voice/ in the repo and they will be used automatically. Any line you skip just falls back to the built-in cartoon sound, so you can do a few at a time.

Only your own voice. Never record audio taken from a TV show, a match or a viral clip — those are owned.
